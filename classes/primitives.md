# Mind Body and Senses

**Status: ESTABLISHED CLASS PRIMITIVES AND CONCEPTUAL FAILURE EXPRESSIONS.** Source: [D03](../sources/post-drowning-workshop-2026-09-25/README.md), sections 8–13. These are not finished abilities or tuned player penalties.

## Warlock — Mind — KNOW EVERYTHING

Mind Suffocation involves overthinking, too many possibilities, false certainty, and failure to distinguish useful information from merely available information. Prioritization degrades.

When the Mind Drowns, focus fails; thoughts interrupt each other or fail to complete. Interpretations multiply faster than they can be resolved. Thought cannot resolve into decision. At pure failure, no actionable thought forms.

Perceiving allies as enemies is a possible severe manifestation, not the universal definition or a generic Confusion mechanic.

**KNOW EVERYTHING → THINK NOTHING.**

## Titan — Body — BEAR EVERYTHING

Body Suffocation makes limbs heavy and movement sluggish. Direction changes become harder; increasing effort produces decreasing movement.

When the Body Drowns, struggling costs more physical capacity than it can produce. It cannot generate enough action to overcome the Pressure acting upon it. At pure failure, the target cannot move.

This is physical failure, not psychological surrender, freezing, suspension, or magical rooting.

**BEAR EVERYTHING → MOVE NOWHERE.**

## Hunter — Senses — SEE EVERYTHING

Sensory Suffocation includes peripheral shimmers that brighten, warbles/waves, and movement that seems delayed or unnaturally fast. Spatial and visual information becomes hard to prioritize.

This is failing perceptual resolution under excessive signal, not time manipulation.

When the Senses Drown, every movement, edge, object, light change, and spatial relationship demands equal attention. The victim receives visual information but cannot resolve where the Hunter—or anything else—is. The Hunter is not invisible.

**SEE EVERYTHING → PERCEIVE NOTHING.**

## Scope

These faculties can Drown independently; they are not all possible Mediums. [Player consequences](../workshop/gameplay-directions.md) remain working examples. [Caveman QA](../workshop/caveman-qa.md) is non-canonical shorthand. Voice remains a [candidate](../workshop/candidates-and-survival.md), not a fourth core faculty.
