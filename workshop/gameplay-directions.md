# Working gameplay directions

**Status: WORKING DESIGN, NOT FINAL KITS OR TUNING.** Source: [D03](../sources/post-drowning-workshop-2026-09-25/README.md), sections 18–19 and 27.

## Pressure-Dense

The established state is binary and positive. The **current working effect** is faster Deep ability regeneration and increased Deep ability damage.

Percentages, duration, stacking, UI, and proc conditions remain unresolved. The working acquisition direction is class/build-specific activity, with possible alternate routes through future Fragments. No Aspect or Fragment design is supplied.

## Successful Offload and ability economy

| Class | Strong working direction |
|---|---|
| Warlock | Likely Grenade Energy |
| Titan | Likely Melee Energy |
| Hunter | Likely Class Ability Energy |

The workshop compares the feel to a Kickstart refund, fueled by moving Pressure rather than Armor Charge. This is an analogy, not copied perk text or a numerical specification.

**HOLD PRESSURE FOR POWER. MOVE PRESSURE FOR TEMPO.**

Energy return follows successful transfer. Pressure must still enter another Medium; Offload is not resource deletion.

## Player Drowning consequences

The shared state should not become one identical generic penalty. **The Pressure/faculty that failed to Offload matters.**

| Class example | Primary working expression | Possible secondary expression |
|---|---|---|
| Warlock | Ability-energy disruption/loss | Mild movement/visual effects |
| Titan | Slowed or failed movement | Mild ability/visual effects |
| Hunter | Mild Darkness/HUD/perceptual disruption | Movement/ability effects |

These are examples, not final penalties/timers or a rule that class identity overrides the failed faculty. The Frost Armor comparison illustrates a shared state with different class interactions; it does not import that mechanic.

Exact Offload implementation, refund values, penalties, released-Pressure representation, and Full-Drowning payoff/boss behavior remain unresolved.
