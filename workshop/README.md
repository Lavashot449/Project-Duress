# Duress workshop and design notes

**SIMPLE TO WIELD. DEEP TO UNDERSTAND.**

[D03](../sources/post-drowning-workshop-2026-09-25/README.md), sections 1 and 31–37, records why Resonance was Drowned: causality-first explanations demanded too much high-level knowledge before the fantasy became intuitive. The clean-sheet question protected no old concept.

Pressure is the player-readable primitive. Mediums, Offload, mastery, and equilibrium can deepen that fantasy without becoming required reading for the surface pitch.
