# Candidates and survival

Source: [D03](../sources/post-drowning-workshop-2026-09-25/README.md), sections 16, 24, and 29–31.

## Explicit survivors

The Deep, clarified as Pressure; Drown in the Deep, independently given the literal meaning of Full Drowning; and Primal Understanding, independently required to explain specialization.

## Candidates

| Idea | Status and boundary |
|---|---|
| Weight of Consequence | Strong survivor candidate: Titan Exotic gauntlet name/visual concept. Old causality perk does not automatically survive; no new perk established. |
| THE DEEP COLLAPSES INWARD | Candidate/surviving Pressure/compression visual idea; no blanket recanonization of old visual laws. |
| Pressure Imprint / negative-space humanoid | Possible surviving visual direction; its old explanation does not automatically carry forward. |
| Voice / SPEAK EVERYTHING | Warlock honorable mention or future escalation: excess expression defeats useful speech. Not a core faculty, verb, Aspect, or mechanic. |
| Hostile Deep-Bearer group | Future concept; unnamed, without complete lore. “Dredgen-like” is shorthand only. |

The Voice phrase “OVERLOAD YOUR VOICE → SPEAK EVERYTHING” is preserved in D03. It does not restore Overload as an active universal state; that term is Suffocation.

## No automatic carryover

Echobound / Anchorforged / Shimmerstalker; What Was / What Is / What Would Be; Permanence / Position / Possibility; Echo / Reverb, Anchor / Pin, Shimmer / Refract; The Perpetual; old causal laws; and old kits/exotics/class mechanics remain historical unless separately re-derived and established.

## Jokes and analogies

“Transference” was a joke, not an approved name. Randal the Vandal as a destination for released Pressure and the Rhulk Offload taunt remain jokes. The universal caveman line—“This is too fucking much for me. Your turn.”—is explanatory shorthand, not canonical dialogue.
