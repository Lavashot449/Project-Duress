## The recovered cycle

```text
WHAT WAS
  → RESONANCE / ILLUMINATION
  → WHAT IS
  → WHAT COULD BE
  → MANIFESTATION
  → NEW WHAT WAS / COMMUNION
```

This is the older guide's exact sequence. The diagram describes the explanatory workflow; it does not mean that perceiving history creates the whole present. The author now aligns Shimmerstalker with **What Would Be**. The relationship between that term and the guide's **What Could Be** requires the full current discussion. This page does not silently substitute one for the other.

## Record and present perception

What Was is the objective record of what happened. Resonance illuminates that record within current actuality. Neither persistence nor illumination provides a causal interpretation for free. A Wielder can attend to an irrelevant event, misunderstand an accurate observation, infer a bad relationship, or attempt an effect beyond their capability.

What Is denotes objective actuality in S03. A belief can exist now while its claim is false: “I believe X” and “X is true” are different propositions. Seeing a historical position does not establish that the target is currently there or already tethered to it.

The guide calls current actuality immutable while allowing movement and change through new actuality. This is not an assertion that the present is frozen forever. The exact temporal transition, latency, or clock step between actuality and What Was was not specified.

## Conceived possibility and Manifestation

In the recovered model, a Wielder conceives What Could Be from their understanding of What Was and What Is. A conceived possibility is not an archived destiny, and conceivability does not prove attainability. A simulation is an actual simulation; it does not make its represented outcome actual.

Manifestation is the universal process by which possibility becomes actuality and yields new What Was. The guide uses an ordinary person sitting down to distinguish participation in that cycle from deliberate Deep-Wielding. A Deep-Wielder deliberately uses Resonance and Primal Understanding within the process. The guide names this distinction without fully deriving its mechanism.

An imagined or attempted event leaves the fact of imagining or attempting in the record. The intended external outcome enters the record only if it actually occurs. A newly manifested result does not retroactively make an unrealized historical alternative have happened.

## Communion and causal evidence

Both successful and failed attempts produce What Was. Communion uses the cycle reflectively: examine what occurred, seek understanding, and attempt again. It is not a separate mechanism that automatically explains failure or guarantees eventual mastery.

If A + B + C accompanies an outcome D, the successful attempt shows that the used combination was sufficient in that instance. It does not establish that every contributor was necessary, that the theory was complete, or that another context will behave identically. Preserve that distinction when turning workshop examples into mechanics.
