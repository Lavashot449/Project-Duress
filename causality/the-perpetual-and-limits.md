# The Perpetual and its supplied limits

**Status: editorial summary of the supplied September 9 governing Books.** Sources: [Rev Grimoire](../books-of-sorrow/rev-grimoire/2026-09-09/README.md) sections 1–2; [Laws of Causality](../books-of-sorrow/laws-of-causality/2026-09-09/README.md) sections 1–5; [Tablets of Ruin](../books-of-sorrow/tablets-of-ruin/2026-09-09/README.md), Tablets 002 and 006. Original texts retain authority.

## Named cycle

**The Perpetual** is the Books' name for What Was → What Is → What Could Be → Manifestation → new What Was. Resonance is the immutable tool that Illuminates the record into present perception; the Wielder supplies selection and interpretation. Illumination neither repeats nor rewrites an old event.

S07's seven foundational laws distinguish immutable What Was, the non-agentic Deep, Resonance that only Illuminates, immutable present actuality, mutable possibility, universal Manifestation, and the return of manifested actuality to What Was. S06 describes the cycle as having no final endpoint. S07 says the cycle may be operated again. Neither statement supplies a guarantee that any particular desired outcome is achievable.

## Knowledge and failure

Immutable first: establish What Was and What Is before treating What Could Be as actionable. Correct Illumination does not guarantee understanding. Success establishes sufficiency rather than perfect causal understanding. A failed path does not grant knowledge that the desired outcome can never occur.

Tradition, doctrine, technique, belief, and interpretation remain mutable. S07 locates the required adaptability in the Wielder, not in the facts. This matters when reading the later [What Would Be / Could Never Be distinction](what-would-be-and-could-never-be.md): the missing explanation cannot be replaced with “an attempt failed, therefore the outcome was impossible.”

## Scope, capability, and exhaustion

S07 explicitly makes complexity depend on target, scope, and the Wielder's understanding/capability. Its comparisons include a Dreg versus Riven, one clone versus fifteen, and a single bullet versus a full six-person damage rotation. These illustrate scale; they do not define numerical thresholds, exact costs, or guaranteed difficulty tiers.

Overreach can fail cleanly and can exhaust the Wielder. Exhaustion can temporarily degrade simpler Manifestations. It does not inherently erase the ability to Wield. The supplied text does not establish universal injury, automatic permanent loss, a quantified recovery timer, or a complete contest rule for incompatible outcomes.

## Drowning and expressions

S07 describes Drowning as personal What Was returning non-agentically to the greater What Was. A coherent survivor distinguishes “That Was” from “I Am.” Survival alone is not mastery. Primal Understanding supplies a fundamental lens; Guardian expressions are Permanence, Position, and Possibility, while Deep-Wielder is the broader category.

The September 9 expression definitions are ECHO — What Was made Manifest; ANCHOR — What Is made Manifest; SHIMMER — What Could Be made Manifest; REVERB — consequential propagation rather than a fourth state. S06 allows learning across classes. These are attributed Book definitions, not an automatic full mechanic specification for the newer names.
