# Current causal grammar and subclass verbs

**Status: CURRENT AUTHOR-CONFIRMED terminology and design grammar; full mechanics remain DRAFT / ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT.** Sources: [S01 freeze](../canon/2026-09-23-freeze.md) and [S09 corrective workshop state](../sources/2026-09-23-resonance-corrective-directive.md).

| Subclass | Domain | Primary verb | Escalation | Grenade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Echobound](../subclasses/echobound.md) | WHAT WAS | ECHO | REVERB | REPRISAL GRENADE |
| [Anchorforged](../subclasses/anchorforged.md) | WHAT IS | ANCHOR | PIN | DEADLOCK GRENADE |
| [Shimmerstalker](../subclasses/shimmerstalker.md) | WHAT WOULD BE | SHIMMER | REFRACT | DIVERGENCE GRENADE |

The primary verb establishes the causal state. The secondary verb escalates or interacts with that established state. Echo → Reverb, Anchor → Pin, and Shimmer → Refract are **verb progressions**, analogous in design grammar to Destiny status progressions. They are not mixed verbs or replacements that remove the primary verb.

Reprisal Grenade replaces the old/basic Echo Grenade terminology. Deadlock Grenade is the established Anchorforged grenade name. Divergence Grenade replaces the older Shimmer/Reverb grenade terminology. Reverb is reserved for the Echo → Reverb escalation, not a grenade name.

The September 9 Books and historical guide retain their earlier propagation definition and What Could Be wording. Do not silently rewrite those sources. Apply current terms in editorial pages and preserve older terms with their provenance. Current naming alone does not approve damage, durations, interactions, or transplantation of old mechanics.

## Current conceptual relationship

What Would Be can become Could Never Be when a once-valid causal branch becomes inaccessible. The fact that the branch was once possible remains What Was; the branch cannot be actualized from the new causal state. **The Deep Persists What Was, not What Never Was.** The [full distinction](what-would-be-and-could-never-be.md) separates that statement from unprovided rules or numerical mechanics.
