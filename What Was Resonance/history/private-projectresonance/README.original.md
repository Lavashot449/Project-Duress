# Project Resonance --- The Echoes

**Project Resonance** is a creative design project by **Manifestation
Studios**, focused on reimagining characters, encounters, weapons,
environments, and rewards through a unified visual and narrative
framework.

Each completed **Echo** is built from individually developed
components---FORM, VOICE, POWER, BOONS, and DOMAIN---before being
assembled into a final **Manifestation**.

## Fully Manifested Echoes

-   **Riven --- Echo of A Thousand Voices**
-   **Rhulk --- Echo of the Deep**
-   **Nezarec --- Echo of Nightmares**

Each Echo presented here represents its completed, approved
manifestation.

## About

Project Resonance combines visual design, narrative writing, asset
composition, and deterministic image processing to explore an alternate
interpretation of familiar worlds and characters.

This repository serves as the public archive and gallery for completed
Echoes.
