10. WHAT NEW REV MUST NOT LOSE

The Deep has no agency. It simply is.

What Was persists; persistence is not sentimentality and does not mean the old self is secretly alive.

Rhulk and Savathûn are the true endpoints because both have a relationship to drowning, but Rhulk reads it as exclusivity while Savathûn reads it as transformation and recognition.

Caiatl believes the Deep-Bearer and rejects what the truth requires, rather than dismissing it as evil.

Crow is the emotional bridge because his Uldren→Crow existence already embodies What Was stripped into What Is.

Riven is fascinated by a wish-result she cannot manipulate normally.

Ikora asks what model of reality must change. Zavala asks what persistent memory costs. Saint asks whether the survivor is still the person.

The newest Savathûn idea is the most important development: the Guardian restoring her memories can be re-read through the Deep-Bearer lens as the Perpetual being performed on Savathûn without the Guardian even trying to out-cunning her.

Lee's phrase: 'Deep-Bearers did as Deep-Bearers do. The Deep Persists, The Perpetual Preserved. We did our job. Our job just happened to be way more cunning that Savathun would have liked it to be.'
