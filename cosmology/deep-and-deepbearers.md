# The Deep and Deepbearers

**Status: DRAFT / ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT — synthesis of the September 9 working architecture, with explicitly frozen statements identified.** Sources: [S01](../sources/2026-09-23-author-directive.md), [S03](../history/workshop-2026-09-09/README.md), and historical [S04](../history/voice-note-2026-09-08/README.md).

## What persists

**Frozen principle: The Deep Persists What Was, not What Never Was.**

The recovered September 9 model describes the Deep as the non-agentic Vessel by which the objective record of What Was persists. Destroying a thing, forgetting an event, or removing its physical traces does not make the event never have occurred. Further actuality produces additional What Was; immutability protects the facts already recorded rather than preventing further events.

The library analogy is an aid to discussion. It does not establish literal shelves, books, search indexes, a curator, or a mind that decides relevance. Nor does the model place unrealized futures in that record.

## Resonance and Illumination

In S03, Resonance is the immutable, non-agentic tool used to Illuminate What Was within What Is. Illumination makes the record presently perceptible. It does not by itself repeat the event, interpret it, recommend a response, or make a desired effect happen.

The Wielder supplies attention, selection, interpretation, and proposed use. A truthful record can still be misunderstood. Finite attention and understanding limit the Wielder; the recovered guide does not describe the Deep choosing which evidence the Wielder deserves.

## Drowning and persistence

S04 explores Drowning as the collapse of the structures by which someone understands themself. Its central discovery is persistence after those structures fail, rather than conquest of a conscious Deep or a prize granted by it. Its explicit rejection of testing, choosing, and taking guards against importing agency into the Deep.

S03 later sharpens the surviving distinction to **“That Was. I Am.”** Personal What Was returns to the greater What Was; it is not destroyed or converted into Never Was. This later working account resolves neither every experiential detail of Drowning nor a final graduation criterion. “Drown deeper” remains a working progression, not a quantified level system.

The voice note also says Resonance “strips away what was.” That historical wording is preserved in its source, but must not be used to override today's persistence principle. See [the decision log](../continuity/decision-log.md).

## Deep-Wielder and Deepbearer

S03 uses **Deep-Wielder** as the broader category and **Deepbearer** for the Guardian expression: all Deepbearers are Deep-Wielders, not the reverse. Surviving Drowning is not automatically equivalent to acquiring the Primal Understanding used for a subclass expression.

The guide associates the Guardian expressions with Permanence (Echobound), Position (Anchorforged), and Possibility (Shimmerstalker). Today's explicit alignments are [What Was, What Is, and What Would Be](../subclasses/README.md). The older conceptual associations are retained as dated working material rather than asserted as an additional September 23 freeze.

The guide gives Wishes, Subjugation, Nightmares, and Multiple Simulations as working examples associated with Riven, Rhulk, Nezarec, and Atheon. These examples do not supply their complete Echo dossiers, establish a final roster, or prove a universal classification of every being.

## Limits that remain open

The recovered model distinguishes access to a record from understanding and capability. It does not fully derive the bridge from understanding to an effect, resolve incompatible Manifestations, quantify attention or capacity, or establish that repeated Communion guarantees mastery. Those gaps remain [ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT](../ACTIVE-DEVELOPMENT.md).
