1. Why Resonance Was Drowned
External review identified that causality required too much high-level knowledge before the subclass power fantasy became intuitive. The problem was not simply wording: Resonance was being explained from metaphysics downward.
DESIGN LAW: SIMPLE TO WIELD. DEEP TO UNDERSTAND.
The clean-sheet question became: If Destiny needed a third Darkness subclass and Project Resonance had never existed, what would we build? Nothing was protected. The answer that emerged was Pressure and the system now called Duress.
2. The Deep
THE DEEP IS PRESSURE.
The Deep is the crushing weight of knowledge or power, exerted through a Medium.
The Deep does not choose.
The Deep does not breathe.
The Deep has no sentience or agency.
It does not reward, punish, judge, recognize worthiness, recognize mastery, or grant permission.
It does not care whether a Wielder says yes or no; technically, 'care' itself is already too agentic.
It does not show mercy. Mercy requires a choice.
It simply releases/exerts Pressure.
THE DEEP HAS NO MORAL ALIGNMENT. MORALITY BELONGS TO THE WIELDER.
3. Pressure and Medium
Pressure is the player-readable primitive. Physical pressure is the easiest analogy: greater depth/load creates greater pressure. This is NOT water magic. Ocean/depth/drowning imagery is an analogy and thematic expression.
CAN PRESSURE BE APPLIED TO IT? YES? IT IS A MEDIUM.
Do not over-taxonomize Mediums. Mind, Body, and Senses are the three current class-relevant faculties, not the only possible Mediums in existence.
4. Darkness Subtraction Through Excess
Duress does not begin by subtracting function. It applies MORE until the Medium exhausts every response it can give.
PROVIDE EVERYTHING, AND SOON NOTHING IS LEFT TO GIVE.
Mind: do not remove thought. Give it more to think.
Body: do not initially remove movement. Give it more Pressure to bear.
Senses: do not blind it. Give it more to perceive.
KNOW EVERYTHING → THINK NOTHING
BEAR EVERYTHING → MOVE NOWHERE
SEE EVERYTHING → PERCEIVE NOTHING
The subtraction is the RESULT of excess. The Deep forces a faculty to give everything until it has nothing left to give.
5. Pressure Flow
PRESSURE IN > PRESSURE OUT → PRESSURE ACCUMULATES
PRESSURE IN < PRESSURE OUT → PRESSURE DISSIPATES
This is underlying lore/mechanical logic, not a player-facing engineering simulator.
Dissipation
Passive, uncontrolled reduction/spreading of concentrated Pressure over time. Real pressure systems lose stored pressure over time.
Offload
Active, intentional transfer of Pressure from one Medium into another. Pressure actually goes somewhere else; Offload is not merely resource deletion.
Release
When a containing Medium catastrophically fails, Pressure is no longer contained and disperses according to circumstance.
RELEASED PRESSURE HAS NO PREFERRED DESTINATION.
It can go to you, me, another Deep-Bearer, another target, the environment, or—workshop joke—Randal the Vandal.
6. Suffocation
The earlier working term `Overload` was explicitly culled because Destiny already has Overload Champions.
SUFFOCATION = PRESSURE ACCUMULATING FASTER THAN IT CAN DISSIPATE OR BE OFFLOADED.
Suffocation is not enemy-only. Deep-Bearers can be Suffocated and can deliberately permit controlled Suffocation while approaching Pressure-Dense.
7. Drowning
A FACULTY DROWNS WHEN ACCUMULATED PRESSURE EXCEEDS ITS FUNCTIONAL CAPACITY.
Drowning is the failure state of Suffocation. It is not automatically death. It persists while enough Pressure remains concentrated to keep the faculty above its failure threshold. Magnitude and precision of applied Pressure affect, in lore, how long Drowning persists. Gameplay may compress this into readable durations.
8. Three Faculties / Class Primitives
WARLOCK — MIND — KNOW EVERYTHING
TITAN — BODY — BEAR EVERYTHING
HUNTER — SENSES — SEE EVERYTHING
These are conceptual primitives, NOT finalized subclass names. Do not restore old Resonance class names automatically.
9. Warlock: Mind
Mind Suffocating
Overthinking.
Too many possibilities demand consideration.
False certainty: believing you know what an enemy will do before they do it.
Believing you know more than you actually know.
Useful information becomes difficult to distinguish from merely available information.
Loss of prioritization.
Mind Drowned
Focus fails.
Thoughts interrupt other thoughts.
Thoughts fail to complete.
Interpretations multiply faster than they can be resolved.
Decision paralysis.
Possible severe manifestations may include perceiving allies as enemies, but this is NOT the universal definition and should not become generic Confusion.
THE MIND CONTAINS SO MUCH THOUGHT THAT IT CANNOT RESOLVE THOUGHT INTO DECISION.
At pure failure, no actionable thought forms.
Caveman QA — non-canon wording
Here, you think for me.
10. Titan: Body
Body Suffocating
Slower movement.
Sluggishness.
Limbs feel heavy.
Movement requires increasing effort.
Changing direction becomes harder.
Increasing effort produces decreasing movement.
Body Drowned
The Body reaches the point where even struggling against Pressure costs more physical capacity than it can produce. This is not psychological surrender.
THE BODY CAN NO LONGER PRODUCE ENOUGH ACTION TO OVERCOME THE PRESSURE ACTING UPON IT.
At pure failure, the target cannot move. It is not frozen, suspended, or magically rooted.
Caveman QA — non-canon wording
Here, carry my fat ass around.
11. Hunter: Senses
Sensory Suffocating
Shimmers appear in peripheral vision.
Shimmers become brighter.
Warbles/waves appear through the air.
Movement may appear delayed or unnaturally fast.
Spatial and visual information becomes increasingly difficult to prioritize.
This is not time manipulation. Perceptual resolution is failing under excessive signal.
Senses Drowned
Every movement, shimmer, edge, object, change in light, and spatial relationship demands equal attention.
YOU SEE EVERYTHING AND YET PERCEIVE NOTHING.
The Hunter is not invisible. The victim may technically receive visual information containing the Hunter but cannot meaningfully resolve where the Hunter—or anything else—is.
Caveman QA — non-canon wording
Be my seeing-eye dog. I cannot see. I'm legally blind.
12. Faculty-Specific Drowning
Mind, Body, and Senses can Drown independently. Two can Drown while the third remains functional; the remaining faculty becomes the target's last meaningful avenue for interacting with the world.
Mind Drowned → meaningful thought/decision fails.
Body Drowned → meaningful movement/action fails.
Senses Drowned → meaningful perception/location fails.
13. Full Drowning / Drown in the Deep
MIND DROWNED + BODY DROWNED + SENSES DROWNED = FULLY DROWNED
No faculty remains to compensate. No meaningful thought. No meaningful movement. No meaningful perception.
EVERYTHING IS PRESSURE.
This is the literal Post-Drowning meaning of `Drown in the Deep`. The phrase survived because the rebuilt system independently gave it a stronger meaning.
Full Drowning is NOT automatic death. The target still exists but has no functioning faculty through which to respond. Recovery can occur as Pressure dissipates. Death may follow from vulnerability or external danger, but is not the definition.
14. Birth of a Deep-Bearer
A Deep-Bearer was not chosen, spared, blessed, judged worthy, granted mercy, or taught by the Deep. They became Fully Drowned: Mind failed, Body failed, Senses failed. Nothing remained except Pressure.
THE BREAKTHROUGH: PRESSURE DID NOT HAVE TO TERMINATE IN THEM.
They discovered Offload.
A DEEP-BEARER IS ONE WHO BECAME FULLY DROWNED AND LEARNED TO OFFLOAD.
The wording 'survived the Deep' was rejected because it can imply mercy/selection. 'Adapted' was superseded: the defining discovery is transfer, not infinite tolerance.
I don't have to be where the load stops.
15. Deep-Bearers Are Conduits
DEEP-BEARERS ARE CONDUITS FOR PRESSURE, NOT IMMUNE CONTAINERS.
A container primarily holds. A conduit allows something to pass through. Deep-Bearers can receive, conduct, direct, and Offload Pressure. They can still Suffocate and Drown.
16. Offload
OFFLOAD = DELIBERATELY TRANSFER PRESSURE FROM ONE MEDIUM INTO ANOTHER.
It is directed and intentional. It is not passive dissipation and not simple resource deletion.
Universal caveman version: This is too fucking much for me. Your turn.
Warlock: You think for me.
Titan: You carry it.
Hunter: You see it.
`Transference` was a joke riff on Transcendence and is NOT canonized.
17. Reclaiming Released Pressure
A Deep-Bearer can deliberately put themselves in the path of released Pressure and take it on again because they are a conduit. Pressure does not 'return to its owner'; the Wielder simply becomes an available/effective conduit.
Natural cycle: carry Pressure → Offload → target Suffocates/Drowns → Pressure releases → Deep-Bearer takes released Pressure on again.
Do NOT prematurely turn this into a specific pickup mechanic. Lore law established; gameplay representation unresolved.
18. Pressure-Dense
Gameplay
Binary positive state. You either have Pressure-Dense or you do not.
Current working effect: Deep abilities regenerate faster and deal increased damage while Pressure-Dense.
Exact percentages, duration, UI, stacking, and proc conditions are NOT established. Do not invent numbers.
Lore
Pressure-Dense is cumulative/dynamic in lore. It is the razor-thin equilibrium where a Deep-Bearer has accumulated enough Pressure to approach Drowning while controlling Offload and dissipation closely enough to remain functional.
PRESSURE-DENSE = CONTROLLED SUFFOCATION HELD IMMEDIATELY BELOW FAILURE.
Too much dissipation → fall below Pressure-Dense.
Too much accumulation → a faculty Drowns.
More Pressure carried → more Pressure available to Offload.
More Pressure carried → greater danger to the conduit.
Gameplay simplifies the continuum into a binary buff.
How It Is Gained
No universal proc condition is locked. Working direction: each class/build becomes Pressure-Dense by doing its class-specific thing, analogous to shared elemental states accessed through different Aspects/Fragments. Fragments may later provide alternate routes.
Lore-wise, a Deep-Bearer becomes Pressure-Dense by deliberately allowing Pressure to accumulate rather than immediately relieving it.
19. Successful Offload / Ability Economy
Successful Offload is intended to feed the class-specific ability economy, analogous in feel to a Kickstart refund but fueled by moving Pressure rather than consuming Armor Charge.
Warlock successful Offload → likely Grenade Energy.
Titan successful Offload → likely Melee Energy.
Hunter successful Offload → likely Class Ability Energy.
These are strong working directions, not finalized numbers or perk text.
HOLD PRESSURE FOR POWER. MOVE PRESSURE FOR TEMPO.
The energy return is a consequence of successful transfer. The Pressure must still go into another Medium.
20. Primal Understanding
Primal Understanding survived the Drowning because the rebuilt system independently required a reason for specialization.
PRIMAL UNDERSTANDING = THE FACULTY THROUGH WHICH A DEEP-BEARER MOST NATURALLY AND PRECISELY UNDERSTANDS, CONDUCTS, AND OFFLOADS PRESSURE.
Warlock → Mind.
Titan → Body.
Hunter → Senses / Perception.
EVERY DEEP-BEARER CAN HANDLE PRESSURE THROUGH ANY VIABLE MEDIUM. PRIMAL UNDERSTANDING DETERMINES NATURALNESS, PRECISION, EFFICIENCY, AND SPEED — NOT EXCLUSIVE PERMISSION.
A Warlock can Offload physical Pressure; a Titan can Offload mental Pressure; a Hunter can handle either. Outside the Primal Understanding, the problem is often rate: the Wielder may know how to Offload it but not fast enough to matter.
21. Mastery
Workshop analogy: a weak magnet can still attract. A strong magnet does not need to chase things; things are naturally drawn into its influence. This analogy describes behavior, not agency.
At sufficient mastery, Pressure expressed through a Wielder's Primal Understanding can naturally route toward/through them because they have become an exceptionally effective conduit.
Novice: Get this Pressure out of me.
Experienced: You. Take it.
Master through Primal Understanding: You. Take it. There.
MASTERY DOES NOT GRANT AUTHORITY OVER THE DEEP.
Mastery increases capacity to conduct/direct Pressure. The Deep still does not recognize, reward, spare, or accept refusal. Greater mastery can mean greater conductivity and therefore greater exposure. Mastery can put a Wielder closer to dangerous loads that must be managed.
22. Cross-Medium / Cross-Class Combat
DROWNING IS A RATE PROBLEM.
If Pressure is applied faster than the target can Offload or dissipate it, it accumulates. This applies to Deep-Bearers.
Example: a Warlock can Offload Body Pressure, but a Titan may pour physical Pressure into them faster than the Warlock can evacuate it. The Warlock does not fail because they are forbidden from handling Body Pressure; they fail because their off-specialty Offload rate is too slow.
Cross-class combat therefore attacks different load-management competencies. Same-class combat can become specialty-versus-specialty; cross-class combat can deliberately pressure a faculty the opponent handles less efficiently.
23. Deep-Bearer vs Deep-Bearer Equilibrium Warfare
A Pressure-Dense Deep-Bearer is deliberately operating near Drowning. Another Deep-Bearer can exploit that equilibrium.
Offload additional Pressure into a Pressure-Dense opponent to push them over a faculty threshold.
Attack a faculty outside their Primal Understanding to exploit slower handling.
Anticipate or contest where the opponent tries to Offload.
Force the opponent to become the terminus where Pressure finally stops moving.
The loser is not necessarily the one with less raw power; it can be the one whose Pressure management is outpaced.
24. Hostile 'Dredgen-Like' Deep-Bearer Splinter Group
A future hostile splinter group concept emerged. They are NOT evil because the Deep is evil. They are evil because they understand exactly how Deep-Bearers work and choose to weaponize that knowledge against other Wielders.
They know a Deep-Bearer is not immune and may be intentionally balancing near Drowning. They can deliberately destabilize that equilibrium, attack off-specialty faculties, and attempt to Drown Mind, Body, and Senses systematically until the victim is Fully Drowned.
THE DEEP DID NOTHING DIFFERENT. THE WIELDER DID.
Do NOT name this group yet. `Dredgen-like` is descriptive shorthand only.
Benevolent Mirror
The exact same knowledge can be used benevolently: take Pressure from someone who cannot carry it, help another Deep-Bearer Offload, or become the better conduit so another Medium does not Drown.
Benevolent: You can't carry that. Give it to me.
Malicious: You're barely carrying what you already have. Here's mine.
25. Magnitude, Precision, and Drowning Duration
Two Deep-Bearers can apply similar total Pressure and produce different results. Mastery has at least two important dimensions:
Magnitude — how much Pressure can be applied.
Precision — how efficiently Pressure can be concentrated into the exact faculty/function intended to fail.
More Pressure plus better concentration means more Pressure must dissipate before the faculty becomes functional again. A weak/rough application may Drown a faculty briefly; a master can potentially sustain the failure far longer in lore.
Do NOT turn this into arbitrary numerical duration claims yet.
26. Deep-Bearers Cannot Simply Refuse Pressure
A master conduit does not gain an opt-out. Pressure comes from the Deep, and the Deep has no agency with which to respect refusal.
THE DEEP RELEASES PRESSURE. IT DOES NOT ASK.
A stronger Primal Understanding can make a Wielder an even more effective route for the exact Pressure most capable of Drowning them. This is a cost of mastery, not a contradiction.
27. Drowning Consequences for Player Deep-Bearers
Gameplay-side Drowning should NOT be flattened into one identical generic penalty for all three classes. The universal state is shared; the consequence depends on the Pressure/faculty that failed to Offload.
Working examples, not final tuning:
Warlock Drowning: ability-energy disruption/loss as the primary expression; possible secondary mild movement/visual effects.
Titan Drowning: slowed or failed movement as the primary expression; possible secondary mild ability/visual effects.
Hunter Drowning: mild Darkness/HUD/perceptual disruption as the primary expression; possible secondary movement/ability effects.
The Frost Armor comparison used in workshop: Frost Armor/taking damage can be universal while different classes wield/interact with it differently. Likewise, Suffocation/Drowning are universal while HOW the class fails or benefits can differ.
SUFFOCATION AND DROWNING ARE UNIVERSAL. THE PRESSURE YOU FAILED TO OFFLOAD MATTERS.
28. Rhulk — Conceptual Retrospective Example
Rhulk was used as a conceptual lens for Full Drowning, NOT as proof that his existing encounter mechanics are literally Duress mechanics.
Mind: remembered/foreign imagery and knowledge can conceptually pressure cognition.
Body: overwhelming beams/attacks and physical force pressure the Body.
Senses: Pervading Darkness and perceptual oppression pressure the Senses.
The convergence of all three gives a useful picture of Full Drowning: cannot think your way out, move your way out, or perceive your way out.
Workshop joke: Rhulk tried to Suffocate our perception; a Deep-Bearer could answer by Offloading. 'You didn't Offload hard enough. Drown.'
Do not rewrite canonical Destiny lore to claim Rhulk literally used the new Duress subclass unless the user later makes that explicit.
29. Voice — Warlock Honorable Mention / Escalation Candidate
A fourth Warlock-adjacent idea emerged:
OVERLOAD YOUR VOICE → SPEAK EVERYTHING.
Concept: a voice forced to express everything eventually cannot produce useful speech. The mouth/voice becomes a Medium buckling beneath too much expression.
This was explicitly left as an honorable mention / possible future Warlock escalation. It is NOT yet promoted to a core faculty, verb, Aspect, or mechanic.
Do not force it into the build early.

31. Naming State
The repository is now `Project-Duress`, and Duress is the current working subclass/element name.
The Deep is NOT the subclass name; the Deep is the underlying Pressure foundation/source concept.
Naming discussion considered words such as Crux, Burden, Duress, Compression, Gravitas, Oppression, and Saturation. Duress became the active working name because it reads cleanly beside Stasis/Strand and conveys pressure exerted upon something.
Do not invent a new name without a new workshop decision.
32. The Surface Pitch vs the Lore
The core failure of old Resonance was putting the engine diagram on the hood ornament. Duress must keep a clean front door.
PLAYER-FACING: PRESSURE. GIVE SOMETHING MORE THAN IT CAN HANDLE UNTIL IT HAS NOTHING LEFT TO GIVE.
The deeper lore may explain Mediums, Suffocation, Drowning, Full Drowning, Offload, Pressure-Dense, Primal Understanding, dissipation, precision, and mastery. A player should not need that dissertation to understand the power fantasy.
