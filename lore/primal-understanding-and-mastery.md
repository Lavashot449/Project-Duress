# Primal Understanding and mastery

**Status: ESTABLISHED LORE FOUNDATION.** Source: [D03](../sources/post-drowning-workshop-2026-09-25/README.md), sections 20–22 and 25–26.

Primal Understanding is the faculty through which a Deep-Bearer most naturally and precisely understands, conducts, and Offloads Pressure: Warlock/Mind, Titan/Body, Hunter/Senses or Perception.

Every Deep-Bearer can handle Pressure through any viable Medium. Specialization determines naturalness, precision, efficiency, and speed—not exclusive permission. A Warlock can Offload Body Pressure but may be outpaced by a Titan applying it.

## Mastery

Mastery increases capacity to conduct and direct Pressure. At sufficient mastery, Pressure expressed through a Wielder's Primal Understanding can naturally route toward/through them because they are an exceptionally effective conduit.

The magnet comparison in the workshop describes behavior. It does not grant agency to Pressure or establish magnetism as a mechanic.

**MASTERY DOES NOT GRANT AUTHORITY OVER THE DEEP.**

The Deep does not recognize skill or respect refusal. Greater conductivity can bring greater exposure and dangerous loads. A master does not gain an opt-out.

## Magnitude and precision

Magnitude is how much Pressure is applied. Precision is how efficiently it is concentrated into the intended faculty/function.

Similar total loads can produce different results. More Pressure plus better concentration leaves more to dissipate before the faculty functions again. Brief versus sustained Drowning is a lore distinction, not an approved duration table.
