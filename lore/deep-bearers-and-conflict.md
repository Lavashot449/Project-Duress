# Deep-Bearers and conflict

Source: [D03](../sources/post-drowning-workshop-2026-09-25/README.md), sections 14–17 and 22–28. Established lore and future concepts are separated below.

## Becoming a conduit — established foundation

A Deep-Bearer became Fully Drowned and discovered that Pressure did not have to terminate in them. They learned to Offload.

They were not chosen, spared, blessed, granted mercy, judged worthy, or taught by the Deep. “Survived the Deep” was rejected for possible implications of selection or mercy; “adapted” was superseded by the specific discovery of transfer.

Deep-Bearers receive, conduct, direct, and Offload Pressure. They are **conduits, not immune containers**, and can still Suffocate and Drown.

## Equilibrium warfare — established lore implications

A Pressure-Dense opponent is immediately below Drowning. Additional Pressure can push a faculty past its threshold. Off-specialty Pressure may arrive faster than their handling can relieve it.

The workshop describes contesting where opponents Offload and forcing them to become the terminus where Pressure stops moving. The loser need not have less raw power; their handling can be outpaced.

These are conceptual combat implications, not completed targeting, interception, or PvP rules.

## Morality — established foundation

**The Deep is morally inert. Morality belongs to the wielder.**

The same knowledge can help someone carry an unmanageable load, help another Deep-Bearer Offload, or make a wielder the better conduit so another Medium does not Drown. It can also destabilize someone already near failure.

## Hostile splinter group — future concept

A future hostile, “Dredgen-like” group would knowingly exploit those vulnerabilities, attack off-specialty faculties, and work toward systematic Full Drowning.

“Dredgen-like” is shorthand, not an approved name. The group's complete lore remains unresolved. Its evil belongs to its choices, not the Deep.

## Rhulk — conceptual retrospective only

The workshop uses remembered/foreign imagery, physical force, and perceptual oppression as a conceptual lens for convergent Mind/Body/Senses failure.

This does not establish that Rhulk's canonical encounter literally uses Duress. The associated Offload taunt is a workshop joke, not locked dialogue or official Destiny lore.
