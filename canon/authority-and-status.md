## Editorial boundaries

Preserve originals verbatim. Put context, warnings, and interpretation in a companion page rather than inside an exact source copy. Give summaries their source IDs and dates. Keep a record of corrections instead of silently harmonizing contradictions.

The September 9 guide is an AI-assisted workshop document, not a verbatim author transcript or a governing Book. Its answers are evidence of the recovered working model. They must not be promoted wholesale to frozen canon. The September 8 transcript contains speech-recognition uncertainty and earlier contradictions; its caveats travel with it.
