# Contributing

Begin with [authority and status](canon/authority-and-status.md). Preserve the author's distinction between canon and workshop. New wording should make the source clearer without silently supplying missing rules.

For each addition, identify its source, date/version, author approval where available, and status. Keep original files unchanged. Put editorial context next to source copies, and record checksums in the manifest. A source gap is not permission to fabricate an item, an ability, a roster member, or a governing text.

For Book imports, follow [the mirror requirements](books-of-sorrow/README.md). For contradictions, add the affected claims and their sources to [the decision log](continuity/decision-log.md), retaining both until resolved. Do not treat an AI-written summary as a substitute for an author decision.

Before committing, check relative links, source hashes, terminology, unresolved-status labels, and the presence of any private operational data unrelated to Resonance. Avoid copying account credentials, entire chat archives, or unrelated personal files. Update the [change log](CHANGELOG.md) and describe the actual boundary of what was validated.
