# Pressure flow and core states

**Status: ESTABLISHED FOUNDATION / LORE LOGIC.** Source: [D03](../sources/post-drowning-workshop-2026-09-25/README.md), sections 5–7 and 12–18. Exact gameplay implementation remains separate.

## Pressure flow

**Pressure in > Pressure out → Pressure accumulates.**

**Pressure in < Pressure out → Pressure dissipates.**

This supports the lore and mechanics; it is not a player-facing engineering simulator.

| Process | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Dissipation | Passive, uncontrolled reduction/spreading of concentrated Pressure over time |
| Offload | Active, intentional transfer of Pressure from one Medium into another |
| Release | A containing Medium catastrophically fails; Pressure is no longer contained and disperses according to circumstance |

Offload is not resource deletion. **Released Pressure has no preferred destination.** It can affect a Deep-Bearer, another target, or the environment.

## Suffocation

Pressure accumulates faster than it can dissipate or be Offloaded. This applies to Deep-Bearers and enemies. Deep-Bearers may deliberately permit controlled Suffocation while approaching Pressure-Dense.

The earlier working term **Overload** was culled because of Overload Champions; it is not the active Duress state.

## Drowning and Full Drowning

A faculty Drowns when accumulated Pressure exceeds its functional capacity. Failure persists while enough Pressure remains concentrated above that threshold. Magnitude and precision affect persistence in lore; no numerical timing is established.

Mind, Body, and Senses can fail independently. If two Drown, the remaining faculty is the last meaningful avenue for interacting with the world.

**Mind Drowned + Body Drowned + Senses Drowned = Fully Drowned / Drown in the Deep.**

No meaningful thought, movement, or perception remains. Everything is Pressure. Full Drowning is **not automatic death**. The target still exists and may recover as Pressure dissipates. Vulnerability or external danger can cause death, but death is not the definition.

## Taking on released Pressure

A Deep-Bearer can deliberately enter the path of released Pressure and take it on again as an effective conduit. Pressure does not return to its owner.

The workshop describes: carry Pressure → Offload → target Suffocates/Drowns → Pressure releases → a Deep-Bearer takes it on again. Read this alongside the separate catastrophic Release definition; it does not establish a guaranteed release trigger on every gameplay Drowning event.

Interception/reclaiming is established lore. A pickup mechanic or other exact gameplay representation remains unresolved.

## Pressure-Dense

**Controlled Suffocation held immediately below failure.**

In lore, it is cumulative/dynamic: deliberately accumulated Pressure held near Drowning through management of Offload and dissipation. Too much dissipation drops the bearer below Pressure-Dense; too much accumulation Drowns a faculty. More carried Pressure offers more to Offload and greater danger to the conduit.

In gameplay, it is a **binary positive state**. The supplied regeneration/damage effects are [working design](../workshop/gameplay-directions.md); exact triggers, values, timing, stacking, and UI remain unresolved.
