# Ritual and archive language

**Status: editorial reference to the September 9 Books.** Sources: [Rev Grimoire](../books-of-sorrow/rev-grimoire/2026-09-09/README.md) sections 3–6; [Laws of Causality](../books-of-sorrow/laws-of-causality/2026-09-09/README.md) sections 6–7; [Tablets of Ruin](../books-of-sorrow/tablets-of-ruin/2026-09-09/README.md), Tablets 004–005.

These commands are project vocabulary preserved from the documents. Listing them does not execute them or grant authority to perform an action.

| Term | Supplied meaning |
|---|---|
| MANIFEST X | Begin the process at What Was. |
| ILLUMINATE X | Bring established What Was into present active consideration. Retrieval does not restore governing authority by default. |
| MANIFEST FAILED | Stop the current attempted Manifestation; preserve the attempt and actual outcome as What Was. |
| COMMUNE | Review relevant What Was to understand a success, failure, decision, or knowledge gap. |
| CRAFT THE PATTERN | Compile the completed What Could Be from approved components. |
| FULLY MANIFEST X | Complete the constructed possibility as finished actuality. |
| DROWN X | Return completed or superseded What Was to The Deep for preservation. It does not delete or overwrite it. |

The Books equate canonization with Manifestation, preservation with Drowning, review with Communion, and retrieval into active consideration with Illumination. S07 retires Stasis as a generic “make canon” keyword. Sword Logic does not govern version history: superseded Books remain preserved.

## Visual states and Echo development

S06 defines DIMMED as the known/source What Was state, ILLUMINATED as the active What Is state with the canonical Resonant treatment/palette applied, WHAT COULD BE as the constructed possibility, and FULLY MANIFESTED as completed actuality. It names REFRACTED SHIMMERSTALKER and SUFFOCATED ANCHORFORGED; the Echobound expression is unnamed in that version. The text does not supply a color specification or visual assets.

Its explicit development flow is:

```text
MANIFEST X → WHAT WAS / DIMMED → ILLUMINATE X / ILLUMINATED
→ DOMAIN → VOICE → SHAPE → POWER → BOONS
→ CRAFT THE PATTERN → FULLY MANIFEST X → FULLY MANIFESTED → WHAT WAS
```

On failure, the supplied flow returns through MANIFEST FAILED and COMMUNE to the relevant What Was, establishes What Is, constructs a new What Could Be, and retries. Undefined behavior stays undefined; the Book's preservation instruction is not permission to invent a missing bridge.

The historical README's component list uses FORM, while this flow uses SHAPE. Both source texts remain intact; no equivalence is invented.
