## Confirmed and newly recovered content

The Books name the universal cycle **The Perpetual**. S06 defines its vocabulary, Guardian expressions, visual states, and ritual flow. S07 states the foundations, epistemic boundaries, Drowning rules, expressions, failure/capacity limits, and preservation rules. S08 records the September 9 development and six P-P footers. They substantiate much of the recovered workshop account without converting the workshop's every example into a Book passage.

S07 adds explicit limits: complexity scales with target, scope, and understanding/capability; overreach can fail and exhaust the Wielder, temporarily degrading simpler Manifestations without inherently erasing the capacity to Wield. That narrows the earlier “limits not recovered” gap. It does not supply numerical tuning, an arbitration rule for conflicting outcomes, or a full derivation of every causal bridge.
